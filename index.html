Numerology and astrology are traditional practices, not modern sciences — no honest practitioner should promise guaranteed outcomes. What they offer is a structured way to reflect on timing, personality, and decisions. Many people find that clarifying. Important health, legal, or financial decisions should still be discussed with the relevant professionals alongside any reading.

Numerology works from the numbers in your date of birth and name — your life path, destiny, and name numbers. Astrology maps the position of planets and nakshatras at your exact time of birth, through your janam kundli. They answer different questions, and many people combine both for a fuller picture.

Your driver number (mulank) is your birth date reduced to a single digit — for example, 25 becomes 2 + 5 = 7. Your life path number (bhagyank) is the sum of every digit in your full date of birth, also reduced to a single digit (some systems keep 11, 22, and 33 as master numbers). This gives you a starting point — a full consultation goes into what these numbers actually mean for your decisions.

Yes. Name numerology assigns a number to each letter, and correcting a name means adjusting the spelling so it supports your birth numbers. This applies to personal names, business and brand names, and baby naming — along with guidance on choosing important dates. Try the free Numerology Name Checker tool on this site for a quick first check before booking a full consultation.

Kundli matching (guna milan) compares two birth charts across temperament, health, and family compatibility. Whether it's necessary is a personal call — many Indian families check it before finalizing a marriage, and it works best as one input alongside actual mutual understanding between partners. Use the Kundli Checker tool on this site, or book a detailed matching consultation.
